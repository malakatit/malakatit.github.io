About Me

Life, for me, is a mix of full plates and full hearts, always juggling something, always grateful for it. I find joy in the simple things: a new recipe I probably won't follow exactly, a trail that makes my legs regret everything the next day, an evening that turns into a good conversation before I know it. Some of my favorite moments happen far from any screen; around a fire, under a sky full of stars, with people who make the wild feel like home. I'm still figuring a lot of things out, but I'm doing it eagerly, one adventure at a time.
